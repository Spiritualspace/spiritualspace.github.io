Truths, Safeguards & Boundaries

A transparent, respectful understanding between seeker and guide

Welcome. To ensure our connection remains clean, respectful, and grounded in mutual trust, this page outlines the natural human boundaries, privacy safeguards, and spiritual truths of this space.

1. The Scope of Vedic Reflections

All chart layouts, time cycles (Mahadashas), and astronomical alignments shared on this site are rooted in traditional Vedic calculations. They are intended for personal guidance, self-reflection, and inner clarity. They point out the cosmic weather patterns of your life, but they do not trap your free will. These insights represent tendencies and potentials rather than absolute assurances or unchangeable outcomes.

2. Honoring the Divine Agency

In accordance with ancient Vedic systems, we honor that human calculations have natural boundaries. No guide or practitioner can achieve absolute certainty or assume 100% correctness, as the supreme Divine power holds the fluid capacity to alter paths, change destinies, and respond to love, prayer, or inner transformation.

3. Personal Accountability & Free Will

The cosmic blueprints provided here are meant to empower your own intellect, judgment, and choices. You retain full personal agency and responsibility for how you choose to process or apply this reflection to your real-world life paths.

4. Professional Boundaries (No Substitution)

The guidance offered here is purely spiritual in nature. It is not an absolute diagnosis or a substitute for specialized professional care. For critical matters requiring medical treatment, legal counsel, or specific equity/stock market financial investment advice, please continue to consult with licensed professionals in those respective fields.

5. The Sacred Trust of Your Data

When you share your birth metrics (exact time, date, and city coordinates), you place a sacred trust in this space. Your personal parameters live securely and confidentially inside our isolated text system to create your charts. We hold total authority over this data and will never sell, trade, or share your birth details or email with any third-party marketing networks. All financial gifts (Dakshina) are handled outside our systems via globally encrypted networks like PayPal or direct local UPI apps.

6. Cross-Border & Voluntary Accord

This space is managed and operated out of India. Because the cycle of giving operates on a voluntary basis (Dakshina), all contributions are made entirely at the seeker's personal discretion as a gesture of reciprocity, and do not constitute a standard commercial transaction.
