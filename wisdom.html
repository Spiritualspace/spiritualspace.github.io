The Way of Jyotish

"Astrology is not a net that binds you; it is a lamp meant to reveal your hidden pathways."

Vedic Astrology, or Jyotish, translates directly to the "Science of Light." It is a profound Vedanga meant to lift the veil of confusion from our consciousness, helping us align our daily actions with higher universal laws.
